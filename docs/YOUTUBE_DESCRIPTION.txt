NeuroTwin: calibrated digital twins of neural organ-on-a-chip cultures

NeuroTwin turns a microelectrode-array recording of a neural chip into a mechanistic digital twin of that chip: a simulated culture whose biophysical parameters are inferred from the recording with amortised simulation-based inference, together with checks that say when the twin can be trusted. Run forward, the twin answers questions the experiment never asked, and says which answers are firm.

Entry to the Kaggle hackathon "AI4S Open Innovation: AI for Life Science" (End-to-End System category).

Key results (public data)
• Calibrated uncertainty: 90% intervals cover 86–92% of true values on held-out simulations
• The twin flags recordings it cannot explain; 22% of 3,049 real windows pass every check
• Each twin reproduces its own recording far better than another recording's twin (91% vs 52% of statistics)
• Halving excitation silences bursting in every posterior draw; halving inhibition raises synchrony by an uncertain amount
• Label-free imaging: nuclei-count error 1.8% (published model 4.8%), with calibrated intervals
